## How this list is made

1. Jobs come from FirstHire's feed of US entry-level roles and internships (company career sites and job boards), refreshed every few minutes.
2. A job is listed only if its employer filed certified H-1B Labor Condition Applications at **wage Level I for new employment**: the filing a company makes to hire someone at entry level.
3. The H-1B numbers come from the Department of Labor's public [LCA disclosure data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) and are refreshed each quarter.

**What the numbers don't say:** an LCA comes before an H-1B petition. It is not a visa approval, some are never used, and a company's record doesn't mean every role is open to sponsorship. Some roles (government, defense) need US citizenship. Always check the posting and ask the recruiter.
